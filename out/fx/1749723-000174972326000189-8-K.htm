Item 8.01. Other Events.

New Fortress Energy Inc. ("NFE" or the “Company”) previously announced that the Company’s Fast LNG unit was offline, following a mechanical issue with the facility’s gas turbine. The liquefaction equipment itself was not affected. Before the outage, FLNG 1 was online approximately 98% of the time in 2026, excluding scheduled maintenance.

Based on the most recent discussions with the turbine manufacturer, the Company now expects work on the turbine to be complete in early December. The Company continues to expect FLNG 1 to return to service in the fourth quarter of 2026. The Company will provide updates regarding the status of FLNG 1’s return to service through Current Reports on Form 8-Ks, press releases, or on the Company’s website.
