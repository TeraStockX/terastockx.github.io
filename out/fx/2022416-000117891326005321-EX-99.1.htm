Silexion Therapeutics Opens First Two Clinical Sites in Germany for Phase 2/3

Trial of SIL204 in Locally Advanced Pancreatic Cancer

 

Ulm University Hospital and TUM University Hospital in Munich are now open to enroll patients,

expanding the trial into the European Union

 

Additional sites in Israel and Germany are expected to open in the near future

 

Grand Cayman, Cayman Islands, November 23, 2026 -- Silexion Therapeutics Corp. (NASDAQ: SLXN) (“Silexion” or the “Company”), a clinical-stage biotechnology company pioneering RNA interference (RNAi) therapies for KRAS-driven cancers, today announced that two clinical sites in Germany are now open to enroll patients in its Phase 2/3 trial of SIL204 in locally advanced pancreatic cancer: Ulm University Hospital, led by Principal Investigator Prof. Dr. med. Thomas Seufferlein, and TUM University Hospital in Munich, led by Principal Investigator Prof. Dr. med. Hana Algül. They are the Company’s first clinical sites in Germany and expand the trial beyond Israel into the European Union.

 

The German sites operate under the authorization the Company received in June 2026 from Germany’s Federal Institute for Drugs and Medical Devices (BfArM), as previously announced, within the European Union Clinical Trials Regulation framework.

 

The trial is a multinational, Phase 2/3 study evaluating SIL204 in combination with standard-of-care chemotherapy versus chemotherapy alone in patients with locally advanced pancreatic cancer (LAPC). The study includes an initial safety run-in cohort of approximately 18 patients, followed by a randomized cohort of approximately 166 patients.

 

The Company expects additional sites in Israel and Germany to open in the near future, joining Tel Aviv Sourasky Medical Center and the two German sites.

 

“Opening our first two sites in Germany takes our Phase 2/3 trial of SIL204 into the European Union and marks an important step in building a multinational clinical program,” said Ilan Hadar, Chairman and Chief Executive Officer of Silexion Therapeutics. “With Ulm University Hospital and TUM University Hospital now open to enroll patients, and additional sites in Israel and Germany expected to follow in the near future, we are building momentum as we continue advancing the clinical development of SIL204 and progressing toward our planned clinical milestones.”

 

About Silexion Therapeutics

 

Silexion Therapeutics is a pioneering clinical-stage, oncology-focused biotechnology company dedicated to the development of innovative treatments for unsatisfactorily treated solid tumor cancers that have the mutated KRAS oncogene, generally considered to be the most common oncogenic gene driver in human cancers. The Company conducted a Phase 2a clinical trial in its first-generation product candidate, which showed a positive trend in comparison to the control of chemotherapy alone, and is now advancing its lead, second-generation, product candidate, SIL204, a small interfering RNA (siRNA), through Phase 2/3 clinical evaluation. Silexion is committed to pushing the boundaries of therapeutic advancements in the field of oncology and further developing its lead product candidate for locally advanced pancreatic cancer. For more information, please visit: https://silexion.com
