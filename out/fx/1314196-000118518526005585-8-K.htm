Item 5.07 Submission of Matters to a Vote of Security Holders

 

On November 19, 2026, The OLB Group, Inc. (the “Company”) completed its annual meeting of stockholders (the “Annual Meeting”). As of the record date of October 9, 2026 (the “Record Date”), 31,199,261 shares of common stock, par value $0.0001 per share (the “Common Stock”), were issued and outstanding and entitled to vote at the Annual Meeting. The number of shares of Common Stock present or represented by valid proxy at the Annual Meeting was 17,477,814 shares of Common Stock, and quorum was achieved. Stockholders were entitled to one vote for each share of Common Stock owned. The following actions were taken at the Annual Meeting:

 

Proposal No. 1: Election of Four Directors

 

The first proposal was the election of four (4) directors each to serve for a one-year term that expires at the 2027 annual meeting of stockholders, or until the election and qualification of their respective successors in office, subject to their earlier death, resignation, or removal. The vote on the proposal was as follows:

 

Name of Nominee  FOR   WITHHELD   BROKER NON-VOTE 
Ronny Yakov   15,815,869    116,669    1,545,276 
Ehud Ernst   15,814,595    117,943    1,545,276 
Amir Sternhell   15,802,855    129,683    1,545,276 
Alina Dulimof   15,808,775    123,763    1,545,276 

 

Each nominee was elected.

 

Proposal No. 2: Ratification of Appointment of Independent Registered Public Accounting Firm

 

The second proposal was the ratification of the appointment of RBSM, LLP, as the Company’s independent registered public accounting firm for the year ending December 31, 2026. The vote on the proposal was as follows:

 

FOR   AGAINST   ABSTAIN 
 16,764,416    704,777    8,621 

 

Proposal No. 2 was ratified.

 

Proposal No. 3: Approval of the Split Amendment to our Certificate of Incorporation

 

The third proposal was the approval of an amendment (the “Split Amendment”) to our Certificate of Incorporation, as amended (the “Certificate of Incorporation”) to effect a reverse stock split of our issued and outstanding Common Stock at a range of between 1-for-2 and 1-for-25 (the “Reverse Split”) and with such Reverse Split to be effected at such time and date, if at all, as determined by the Board in its sole discretion (provided that it is effected within one year of the date on which the stockholders of the Corporation approve the Reverse Stock Split). The vote on the proposal was as follows:

 

FOR   AGAINST   ABSTAIN   BROKER NON-VOTE 
 16,299,056    893,253    285,505    0 

 

Proposal No. 3 was approved by a majority of the votes cast.

 

Proposal No. 4: Approval of the Fourth Amended and Restated 2020 Share Incentive Plan 

 

The fourth proposal was the approval of the Fourth Amended and Restated 2020 Share Incentive Plan to increase the shares of Common Stock available for issuance under the 2020 Plan from 3,000,000 to 6,000,000 shares of Common Stock. The vote on the proposal was as follows:

 

FOR   AGAINST   ABSTAIN   BROKER NON-VOTE 
 15,408,393    512,096    12,049    1,545,276 

 

Proposal No. 4 was approved by a majority of the votes cast.
