Item 8.01. Other Events.
 
On November 23, 2026, the Company announced that it received written feedback from the U.S. Food and Drug Administration ("FDA") in response to the Company's Type C meeting request regarding the development program for PH-762. The FDA indicated that key components of the Company's nonclinical and clinical pharmacology strategy appear reasonable to support continued development and a future marketing application, subject to final review. The Company remains on track to initiate its planned randomized, placebo-controlled Phase 2b study of PH-762 in the first quarter of 2027.
 
The full text of the press release is attached hereto as Exhibit 99.1 and incorporated herein by reference.
