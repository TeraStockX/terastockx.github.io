Item 1.01. Entry into a Material Definitive Agreement.

 

On November 19, 2026 (the “Effective Date”), Nocera, Inc. (“Nocera” or the “Company”) entered into a letter of intent (the “LOI”) with BitRock Ltd. (“BitRock”), a corporation incorporated under the laws of the Province of Alberta, Canada, regarding a proposed cash investment by the Company in BitRock to fund construction of an artificial intelligence data center with an IT load of approximately 10 MW in Alberta, Canada (the “Project”).

 

Under the LOI, and subject to the conditions described below, the Company would subscribe in cash for newly issued shares of BitRock for an investment amount of approximately US$22,000,000, based on a pre-money valuation of BitRock of approximately US$28,000,000 and a post-money valuation of approximately US$50,000,000. The Company’s resulting ownership would equal its investment amount divided by BitRock’s post-money valuation, or approximately 44%. If the results of the Company’s due diligence differ materially from information provided by BitRock, the pre-money valuation is to be adjusted, and either party may request an independent valuation. The investment proceeds are to be used solely for construction of the Project. The instalments and disbursement conditions will be set out in a definitive investment agreement (the “Definitive Agreement”).

 

BitRock would be responsible for construction management and day-to-day operations of the Project. As a shareholder, the Company would have the right to appoint directors, receive periodic financial reports and participate in decisions on major matters, as further set out in the Definitive Agreement. BitRock has represented that certain customers have entered into memoranda of understanding with BitRock for hosting services. This information has not been independently verified by the Company and is subject to the Company’s due diligence. BitRock has also estimated that the Project’s hosting revenue will have a net profit margin of approximately 25% to 30%, which estimate has not been independently verified by the Company and is subject to the Company’s due diligence.

 

The LOI is legally binding upon execution, and the parties have agreed to execute the Definitive Agreement in good faith as soon as practicable on the terms of the LOI. The Company’s obligation to make the investment is subject to conditions precedent, including (i) completion of due diligence with results reasonably satisfactory to the Company, (ii) execution of the Definitive Agreement, (iii) each party’s receipt of all necessary internal corporate approvals and regulatory approvals, and (iv) the accuracy of information provided by BitRock and the absence of any material adverse change in BitRock’s business, assets or financial condition since the Effective Date.

 

The LOI is non-exclusive and contains confidentiality, non-circumvention and non-solicitation covenants. Each party bears its own expenses. The LOI has a term of sixty (60) days from the Effective Date, which may be extended by thirty (30) days with the written consent of both parties if due diligence is still in progress. The LOI may be terminated by mutual written agreement, by the Company if the conditions precedent have not been satisfied within the term, or by a non-breaching party following a material breach that is not cured within fifteen (15) days after notice. Neither party is liable for damages upon a termination by mutual agreement or by the Company for failure of the conditions precedent. The LOI is governed by the laws of the Province of Alberta and the federal laws of Canada applicable therein. Disputes are to be resolved by arbitration in Calgary, Alberta.

 

There can be no assurance that the Definitive Agreement will be executed or that the investment will be completed on the terms described above or at all.

 

The foregoing description of the LOI does not purport to be complete and is qualified in its entirety by reference to the full text of the LOI, which is filed as Exhibit 10.1 to this Current Report on Form 8-K and incorporated herein by reference.
