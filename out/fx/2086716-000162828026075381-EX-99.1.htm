Standard Nuclear Submits Final Documented Safety Analyses to U.S. Department of Energy for Tennessee and Idaho Fuel Production Facilities

Building on prior DOE regulatory progress, these submissions position the Company to achieve decisive licensing milestones for both Hazard Category 2 facilities

OAK RIDGE, Tenn. – November 23, 2026 – Standard Nuclear, Inc. (NYSE: STDN) (“Standard Nuclear” or the “Company”), a reactor-agnostic producer of TRISO nuclear fuel and the only U.S. company with industrial-scale TRISO fuel fabrication facilities, today announced that it has submitted the Documented Safety Analyses ("DSAs") for both SN-TN in Tennessee and SN-ID in Idaho to the U.S. Department of Energy ("DOE") for review. The submissions mark a key step toward licensing both of the Company’s Hazard Category 2 ("HC-2") fuel production facilities.

Standard Nuclear submitted the DSA for SN-TN on November 6, 2026, as noted in the Company’s November 12, 2026 earnings release, and submitted the DSA for SN-ID on November 12, 2026. Both submissions remain subject to review and approval by DOE.

In July 2026, DOE approved the Preliminary Documented Safety Analyses ("PDSAs") for both facilities. The DSA submissions continue the progress of SN-TN and SN-ID through DOE’s regulatory process. The SN-ID submission follows the same construct as SN-TN, reflecting the templated, replicable design of the two facilities.

Securing DSA approval would mark a critical milestone and position the Company to fulfill existing 2027 fuel production orders. The new sites build on Standard Nuclear’s established operations and regulatory approvals. The Company already conducts DOE-authorized HALEU TRISO fuel production at its SN-0 facility in Oak Ridge, Tennessee, and has secured Nuclear Regulatory Commission ("NRC") approval for TRISO fuel production in Richland, Washington, through its joint venture with Framatome.

Bringing SN-TN and SN-ID online would significantly expand Standard Nuclear’s production capacity, creating a geographically distributed supply chain to ensure fuel availability will not bottleneck advanced reactor deployments.

“Submitting the Documented Safety Analyses for SN-TN and SN-ID demonstrates our ability to deploy TRISO production facilities at the speed and scale the market demands,” said Kurt Terrani, President and Chief Executive Officer of Standard Nuclear. “By replicating an essentially identical facility design, our team achieved near-simultaneous DSA submissions and is advancing both sites in parallel, capturing immediate efficiencies across both builds. We look forward to working closely with the DOE during its review to bring both facilities into service.”

About Standard Nuclear
Standard Nuclear’s mission is to reliably deliver the essential building blocks of nuclear power at scale, enabling cost-effective, safe, and secure energy. Headquartered in Oak Ridge, Tennessee, the Company is focused on the large-scale production of advanced nuclear fuel and power systems. As the only U.S. company with industrial-scale TRISO fuel fabrication facilities today, Standard Nuclear produces a robust, high-performance fuel essential to advanced nuclear reactors for terrestrial, national security, and space applications. The Company’s internationally recognized technical team, together with its operation and ownership of facilities licensed for


large-scale TRISO fuel manufacturing, positions Standard Nuclear to deliver advanced nuclear fuel to a rapidly growing industry and to strengthen the domestic supply chain underpinning U.S. national security. For more information, visit https://www.standardnuclear.com.
