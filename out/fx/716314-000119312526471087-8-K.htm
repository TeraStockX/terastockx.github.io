Item 7.01.

Regulation FD Disclosure.

On November 23, 2026, Graham Corporation (the “Company”) issued a press release announcing its acquisition of Hauschild GmbH & Co. KG and Hauschild SpeedMixer GmbH, the parent company of Hauschild SpeedMixer, Inc., the recognized pioneer of Dual Asymmetric Centrifugal mixing technology (the “Acquisition”). Additionally, the press release included updated fiscal 2027 Net Sales and Adjusted EBITDA guidance. The press release is furnished to this Current Report on Form 8-K as Exhibit 99.1.

On November 23, 2026, the Company posted a slide presentation on its website at www.grahamcorp.com disclosing additional information on the Acquisition. The slide presentation is furnished to this Current Report on Form 8-K as Exhibit 99.2.

The information furnished pursuant to this Item 7.01, including Exhibit 99.1 and Exhibit 99.2 shall not be deemed “filed” for purposes of Section 18 of the Securities Exchange Act of 1934, as amended (the “Exchange Act”), or otherwise subject to the liabilities under such section and shall not be deemed to be incorporated by reference into any filing of the Company under the Securities Act of 1933, as amended, or the Exchange Act.
